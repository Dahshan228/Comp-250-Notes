# Note 10: The Digital Divide Between Defenders

**Quotation:** “However, AI will also aid system owners and software developers in securing systems. As there is a remote chance of universal access to AI for cyber security defence by 2027, there will almost certainly be a digital divide between systems keeping pace with AI-enabled threat, and a large proportion that are more vulnerable” (National Cyber Security Centre 2025, under “Assessment”).

**Commentary:** This passage changed how I think about our research question.
We’ve been treating “defenders” as one group, but the NCSC says they’re going to split.
It gives only a “remote chance,” the lowest level on its scale, that all defenders will have AI by 2027, while criminals will sell AI hacking tools “as a service” (National Cyber Security Centre 2025).
So the real gap may be between attackers and the weakest defenders, like small businesses, local governments, and old websites nobody keeps up with.
PHP runs a lot of exactly those, including WordPress sites and shared hosting (Ntsele 2026).
Our claim that organizations need to respond within hours applies to everyone, but only some of them will actually be able to.
I want our paper to cover what everyone else can realistically do, like taking sites offline the way governments did during PHP2Shell.

**Subject terms:** digital divide; offense–defense balance; patch window
