## Part 1: Topic, Question, and Problem

We are studying how artificial intelligence is speeding up hacking, both in finding security flaws and in carrying out attacks.
One example we seen recently that made us lean twords this topic is "php2shell," a pair of flaws in the PHP programming language that became public in October 2026.
Researchers used AI tools to find these flaws, even though they had been hidden in PHP's code for more than ten years.
When the PHP Foundation released emergency fixes on October 21, 2026, governments in Europe and elsewhere took websites offline while they updated.
We also want to find out whether AI helps defenders who find and fix flaws., and if its more or less than it helps attackers who exploit them.
We also want to know how AI changes the time that organizations have to notice an attack or install a fix.
This question matters because security experts usually assume that website owners have days or weeks to patch a flaw after it becomes public.
If AI lets attackers build a working attack within hours of a fix that assumption no longer holds.
Many organizations that run PHP, from governments to small businesses, need to understand how to respond.
